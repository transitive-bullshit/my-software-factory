# The AI Engineer's Creed

[![The AI Engineer's Creed, a parody scene from Full Metal Jacket: the title over the drill instructor, Jensen Huang in a leather jacket and campaign hat](docs/poster.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GT9zYmPhSys)

> Full Metal Jacket's barracks creed, recast for AI engineering: "This is my software factory."

**[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GT9zYmPhSys)** · [Spin-off: the McGurk effect in AI lip sync](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VAw_z6oKpQ)

AI engineers on X are the recruits and Jensen Huang is the drill instructor. Every line is rewritten to fit the original's timing, then performed again: the recruits' creed is one sung chorus take (Eleven Music), and Jensen's orders are a voice clone (Fish Audio). Every shot is remade with the cast, so none of the film's footage is used: Nano Banana Pro keyframes, H3 Max animation with each speaker's lines pinned to his mouth, and lip sync passes where a mouth needed more (Fal Sync-3, H3 Lip Sync). The title card, word-timed captions and end credits are rendered locally in [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com).

Status: published, 76.1 s including captions and credits. Built with Claude Code (Opus 5.5).

## The original

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkGIxGdZoYY"><img src="https://i.ytimg.com/vi/nkGIxGdZoYY/maxresdefault.jpg" width="480" alt="The barracks creed scene from Full Metal Jacket, the source of the parody"></a>

The parody follows [the barracks creed scene from Stanley Kubrick's *Full Metal Jacket* (1987)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkGIxGdZoYY) beat by beat, from the drill instructor's first order to the fire watch's last line, with every new line timed to the original's.
